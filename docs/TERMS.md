# Terms of use — pilot draft

**This is a draft for product planning, not legal advice or final terms.** A qualified lawyer should adapt it to the actual company, jurisdiction, age requirements, consumer protection, employment, privacy, and marketplace rules before accepting public users.

## Early pilot

Crews & Gigs is an early-stage service for discovering and posting media-production opportunities. Features may change or be unavailable. The pilot currently supports accounts, gig posts, applications, and reports only. It does not verify a person's identity or credentials, guarantee a gig, act as an employer or agent, process payments, or mediate contracts. Equipment transactions, direct messaging, and community chat are not currently offered.

## Member responsibilities

Use accurate account and listing details. You are responsible for checking the other party, agreeing scope/rates/payment/cancellations in writing, and complying with applicable law. Do not post illegal, discriminatory, fraudulent, unsafe, or misleading content; impersonate another person; misuse personal data; or interfere with the service.

## Moderation and availability

Members can report gigs. The operator may review, hide, or remove content and restrict accounts for safety or policy reasons, subject to a fair review process. The pilot is provided without a guarantee of uptime, matching, hiring, payment, or a particular response time. The operator must publish monitored support details and a reporting SLA before launch.
