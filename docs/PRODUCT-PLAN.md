# Crews & Gigs — pilot product plan

## Pilot decision

**Recommended first market:** Lagos, Nigeria, with Remote opportunities visible. This is a proposed pilot geography based on the founder being in Lagos; confirm it before recruitment. Do not advertise worldwide coverage until support and moderation can handle it.

**First user outcome:** a producer can post a clear media gig and a crew member can discover it, apply with a short note, and receive a response.

## Phase 1: gig board

Included in this pilot build:

- Member sign-up and sign-in with a public name, discipline, and city
- Open gig board with keyword, location, and discipline filters
- Gig posting, applications, poster-only applicant view, and closing a post
- Report-a-listing flow for spam, suspected fraud, unsafe/illegal work, harassment/discrimination, or other concerns
- Quick safety copy and basic moderation queue records

Not yet included: verified identity badges, email verification, messaging, hiring/booking, ratings, payments, escrow, equipment listings/rentals, or public forum/chat. Do not claim that talent is verified or that bookings/payments are handled by the platform.

## Phase 2: gear locker

After pilot feedback: equipment profiles, condition and availability, location-based search, rental/sale terms, deposit/payment rules, handover checklist, cancellation and damage disputes, and moderation for prohibited goods. Test with trusted local rental houses before enabling public peer-to-peer transactions.

## Phase 3: community and support

Add moderated discussions and direct messages only after moderation coverage, reporting escalation, privacy controls, abuse-blocking, and response-time expectations are resourced.

## Pilot success criteria

Review weekly. Initial targets are hypotheses to revise after interviews:

- 20–30 opted-in Lagos media professionals complete profiles
- 10 real, quality-checked gig posts from identifiable organizations or producers
- At least 50% of posts receive one relevant application within seven days
- At least 5 completed interviews with posters and applicants about trust and workflow
- Zero unresolved safety reports at the end of each pilot week

Track activation (profile completed), time to first relevant application, qualified applications per gig, poster response rate, repeat posters, report volume/resolution time, and user-reported trust. Avoid collecting sensitive data or tracking people across sites.

## Name and launch checks

Before buying a domain or publicly promoting the mark, have the founder check the exact name, close variants, and relevant media/marketplace classes with a qualified trademark professional. Check registrar availability and social handles directly; this repository cannot guarantee availability or legal clearance. Secure the final domain and social accounts, then update canonical URLs, email addresses, metadata, and launch materials.

## Go / no-go checklist

- Founder confirms pilot geography, eligibility, minimum age, and contact/support owner
- Sample listings removed; only consented, current posts displayed
- Moderators assigned and a response SLA set for abuse/safety reports
- Legal reviewer approves privacy notice, terms, community rules, and any local obligations
- HTTPS domain, persistent database volume, backups, monitoring, and restore test configured
- Email verification and account recovery implemented before broad public registration
- Manual smoke test: register, log in/out, post, search, apply, view applications, close gig, report gig
- Pilot participants consent to research and know the service is an early pilot
