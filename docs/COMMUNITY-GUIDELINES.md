# Community and marketplace safety guidelines — review draft

**This is a product draft, not legal advice.** Have a qualified local reviewer adapt it before opening public registration.

Crews & Gigs is for respectful professional collaboration across media and production. Treat members with respect; be accurate about identity, credits, dates, scope, budget, location, and availability; and get written agreement on deliverables, rates, cancellation, travel, and payment before work starts.

## Not allowed

- Fraud, impersonation, phishing, spam, fake opportunities, or misleading rates
- Discrimination, harassment, threats, doxxing, or unwanted sexual conduct
- Illegal, unsafe, exploitative, or non-consensual work or listings
- Requests to pay a fee to apply for a job or to share passwords/one-time codes
- Listings for stolen, counterfeit, prohibited, or unlawfully obtained equipment
- Sharing another person's contact details, images, or work without permission

## Safety steps

Check the poster's identity and references independently. Agree the work scope and payment terms in writing. Meet in a public/professional location where appropriate. Do not send deposits, equipment, identity documents, or account credentials to an unverified party. The current pilot does not process payments, guarantee job completion, or verify members.

## Reporting and moderation

Use the gig report action for suspected fraud, unsafe work, discrimination/harassment, or other policy concerns. Reports are private to moderators. A report being submitted is not a guarantee of an immediate response; the pilot operator must assign a real monitored contact and response time before launch. Moderators may hide a post while reviewing it, ask for evidence, restrict accounts, or escalate credible threats to appropriate services. Do not retaliate against reporters.

The pilot operator should document decisions, restrict report access, retain only necessary evidence, provide an appeal/contact route, and publish a response SLA before opening the service.
